Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The Pale Android Studio LLC ("we", "us") makes apps, including StockPivot. This policy explains what information our apps handle. In short: we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and our apps don't require an account.

Information we don't collect

Our apps do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, or other personal information, and do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your watchlists, symbols and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple may sync this data across your devices through your own iCloud account. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Market data requests

To show quotes and charts, the app requests market data from our servers or data providers. These requests include the ticker symbols being looked up and, as with any internet request, your device's IP address. We use this only to deliver data, keep the service running and prevent abuse. Server logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to identify you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We receive information from Apple about the status of a subscription, but not your payment details.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
