Support

Need help with one of our apps, or found a bug? Email us and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Email: dev@paleandroidstudio.com

Helpful details to include

  • The app name and version (Settings › About inside the app)
  • Your device model and iOS / iPadOS version
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account. To manage or cancel, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
